About me

Alpha doesn’t sit still. Every edge that works gets copied, published or priced, and then it decays. So the goal isn’t one clever idea. It’s a system that keeps producing them: one that reads deeper, asks harder questions and interprets what it finds better than the marginal holder of the stock.

That means playing a different game. Not calling the next quarter faster than the pods, but understanding businesses, and the people who own them, well enough that the returns don’t depend on what the market does.

AI is the speed tool. The judgement stays mine.

  • scalableagents give the width, I give the depth
  • deepercommodity questions in minutes, the hours go to the hard ones
  • evolvingevery lens is tested, retired or replaced as the edge decays
  • uncorrelatedidiosyncratic by design: paid for being right, not for where the market goes
